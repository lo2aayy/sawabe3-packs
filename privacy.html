Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026
The short version: Fingz has no accounts and no login. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information about you. The game runs on your device. Purchases are handled by Apple and Google, not by us.

1. Who We Are

Fingz ("the App", "we", "us") is a local, offline party game. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

The App does not require an account, email, phone number, name, or login of any kind. We do not collect personal identifiers, we do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not build advertising profiles about you.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

Game settings (sound, vibration, language), scores, and your purchase/unlock status are stored locally on your device. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us. Removing the App removes this data.

4. Camera, Microphone, and Photos

At the end of a game, the App can take a photo or record a video of the players as part of the "dare" feature. This is optional and only happens when you choose to use it.

  • Captured photos/videos are created on your device.
  • We do not upload, store, or receive this media.
  • Media is only shared if you yourself choose to share it using your device's share options.

5. Purchases

The App offers paid access (a one-time lifetime unlock, and time-limited plans including an auto-renewing monthly subscription). All payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment card details. Your purchase status is validated through the store and kept on your device.

6. Question Content

The App downloads question packs from our content server. These requests deliver game content to your device and do not send us any personal information about you.

7. Contact / Feedback

If you use the in-app "Contact & Feedback" form, you voluntarily provide an email address and a message so we can reply. This information is used only to respond to you and is sent through our form delivery provider. Do not include sensitive personal information in feedback messages.

8. Advertising

The free version of the App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, AdMob may access a device advertising identifier and limited device information. Users who purchase premium access do not see ads.

In the European Economic Area and other regions that require it, we show a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads are used, and on iOS we request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you do not consent, you will still see ads, but non-personalized ones. You can change your choices later in your device settings.

For more information on how Google uses advertising data, see Google's Privacy & Terms at policies.google.com.

9. Children

Fingz is a party game intended for a general audience of teens and adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. The game contains playful "dares" and is rated accordingly on each store.

10. Third-Party Services

  • Apple App Store / Google Play: process purchases and subscriptions.
  • Form delivery provider: delivers messages you send through the in-app feedback form.
  • Google AdMob: serves ads in the free version (see section 8).

These services are governed by their own privacy policies.

11. Your Rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. For any question about privacy, contact us below.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
